FLIGHT SCHOOL

Momentum is not a suggestion.

Fly

W / S apply forward or reverse thrust. A / D rotate. Gravity never takes a break.

Survive

Space fires. Shift boosts thrust; Ctrl makes it precise. Collisions and escaping the arena end your run.

Try again

Enter restarts. M toggles sound. Esc exits. The desktop interface also has clickable mute and restart controls.
